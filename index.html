KYIV’S DRONES STRIKE AT SEA

President Zelenskiy confirmed Ukraine hit a Russian cargo ship in the Gulf of Finland with a drone on Saturday, as Norway urged caution over the widening campaign. In a second blow at sea, Ukraine said it sank a Russian warship in a Libyan port.
